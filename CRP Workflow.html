Why three generations of CRUs are on site at once

Building, cold testing and packing one CRU takes three weeks. So every week the team builds two new CRUs (A and B), tests last week's pair (A′, B′) and packs the pair from two weeks ago (A″, B″). Follow CRU A through its three weeks; click a stage to see it on the map.
